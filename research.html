RESEARCH PROGRAM

Research

My research examines physiological mechanisms that connect sleep-disordered breathing with vascular and cardiovascular health.

AREA 01

Obstructive sleep apnea and cardiovascular health

Obstructive sleep apnea involves recurring changes in breathing during sleep. My work is interested in how different physiological features of these events may contribute to cardiovascular consequences, and how treatment approaches might be better matched to individual physiology.

AREA 02

Endothelial and vascular function

This area focuses on vascular responses and endothelial function, including how mechanical and physiological stimuli may influence vascular regulation.

AREA 03

Cerebrovascular function

This work examines cerebrovascular regulation and reactivity, with an emphasis on understanding how sleep-related physiological stressors may intersect with brain health.
